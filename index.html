About Northern Flavours

Northern Flavours Restaurant is your home for delicious, freshly prepared meals in Dutse, Jigawa State. From our kitchen at Gajambe Plaza, our talented chefs & professional cooks serve jollof rice, grilled chicken, shawarma and generous family packages — made to order, just the way you like it.

Enjoy your meal your way: dine in, take away, or let us deliver to your doorstep in about 20 minutes.
